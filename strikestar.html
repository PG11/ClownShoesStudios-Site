Strike Star

A top-down, neon-soaked, single-player space shooter with an arcade soul. Fight alongside your fleet in Conquest, capturing points across the map to destroy the enemy, or play Core Run: 6v6 matches where you battle to launch the core into the enemy's Strike Star.

Every ship. Every fight.

Fly the whole fleet

Nimble fighters, torpedo bombers, beam-cannon destroyers and lumbering battleships, each with real inertia and its own weapons and special ability. Swap ships as the battle changes.

Break the Strike Star

Each side is built around a Strike Star, a colossal station that builds its fleet and defends itself with beams and turrets. Destroy theirs to win. Lose yours and it's over.

Lead your wing

Recruit wingmen and give them orders on the fly, while smart AI fleets fight across the map around you on Easy, Normal or Hard.

Two ways to play

Conquest

Capture points across the map earn credits. Spend them on bigger ships, set your team's production, and build up to an all-out assault on the enemy Strike Star.

Core Run

One glowing core, two fleets. Grab it, pass it between teammates, fight through the defence and hurl it into the enemy Strike Star to score.

Features

  • Intense team battles with full AI fleets on both sides
  • Four ship classes, plus four experimental craft to unlock
  • Conquest and Core Run game modes
  • Wingmen you can recruit and command
  • Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty
  • 43 achievements, with Epic Games Store achievements
  • Glowing neon vector style
  • Full controller support with Xbox and PlayStation button prompts; keyboard with rebindable controls
