About the game

StrikeStar is a fast, top-down space combat game of huge team battles, drawn in a glowing neon vector style.

Each side is built around a Strike Star, a colossal battle station that constructs its fleet. Destroy theirs to win; lose yours and the battle is over. Players fly fighters, bombers, destroyers and battleships with real inertia, switching ships as the fight changes.

In Conquest, capture points earn credits to buy bigger ships. In Core Run, players carry, pass and fight for the core, then hurl it into the enemy Strike Star.

Features

  • Intense team battles with full AI fleets on both sides
  • Four ship classes, plus four experimental craft unlocked by mastering each one
  • Two game modes: Conquest and Core Run
  • Recruit and command wingmen
  • Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty
  • 43 achievements, with Epic Games Store achievements
  • Full controller support, plus keyboard and mouse with rebindable controls
